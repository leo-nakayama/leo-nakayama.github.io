[Setting]
A Japanese AI development firm exhibiting high-context political behavior.

[Actors]
- Manager A (performative manager)
- Manager B (development-side rational actor)
- Engineer L (middle-layer observer)

[Observation]

email-stage political maneuvering.

[Hidden Rules]
- Decisions cannot be decided “too smoothly.”
- Manager A must perform authority regardless of outcome.

[Contradictions]
- Authority ≠ Decision-making
- Responsibility ≠ Control
