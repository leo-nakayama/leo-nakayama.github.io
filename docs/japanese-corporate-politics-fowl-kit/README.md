# FOWL Research Kit: Japanese High-Context Organizational Politics
A modular research kit for anthropologists, sociologists, organizational
scholars, and cross-cultural researchers investigating the political,
ritualistic, and theatrical structures of Japanese corporate life.

This kit integrates:
- **High-context cultural theory**
- **Ritualized organizational chaos analysis**
- **FOWL inference modules (YAML)**
- **Ethnographic case files**
- **Download-ready academic PDFs**

## Purpose
To provide researchers with a computational and theoretical framework to
analyze Japanese organizations as **systems of theater, ritual, and symbolic
politics**, grounded in high-context cultural dynamics.

## Included Components
### 🔹 theory/
Six theoretical essays explaining the core cultural mechanisms:
- High-context politics
- Ritualized chaos
- Performative management roles
- Kabuki-style chabudai gaeshi
- Bunraku backstage logic
- Meta high/low-context comparisons

### 🔹 fowl-modules/
FOWL YAML modules for structured inference.

### 🔹 cases/
Ethnographic-style field notes derived from realistic Japanese corporate
phenomena.

### 🔹 export/
PDF exports ready for citation in academic work.

## Suggested Use
Researchers may:
- Use FOWL for computational ethnography
- Integrate theory files into coursework
- Apply ObservationReport to fieldwork data
- Cross-analyze Japanese firms with global institutions
